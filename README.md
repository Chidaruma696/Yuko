## 📲 Download

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/Chidaruma696/Yuko/releases/latest) and download the `Yuko-x.y.z.apk` file.
2. Open it on your phone. Android will ask for permission to install apps from this source; accept it once.
3. Done. Later versions install on top without losing your library or downloads.

Yuko is not on the Play Store and never will be; it is distributed only from here. And as long as Android stays open, that is enough ([why it matters](#-keep-android-open)).

## 🗺️ What it is

Yuko is [Watanuki](https://github.com/Chidaruma696/Watanuki)'s master: the same idea, applied to manga. It takes the community parsers from [Kotatsu](https://github.com/kotatsu-redo/kotatsu-parsers-redo), **compiles them into the APK** (no loose extensions to install), wraps them in a manga-styled interface, and gives them a reader built for right-to-left reading.

| 📖 Read | 📥 Save | 🎨 Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home with "Continue reading," recommendations based on your genres, latest updates, and most popular per source | Parallel downloads, per chapter or full series, with free-space checks | Ten palettes inspired by Touhou characters, in light and dark |
| Right-to-left reader by default, fit to screen height, with zoom, vertical mode for webtoons, and tap-zone controls | Offline reading of everything you saved | "Manga" design system ported from Komi Store: paper, ink, stamps, and screentones |
| Library with favorites, read later, and history with per-page progress | Queue with progress and retries | 18+ filter **off by default** and excluded-genre filter from the very first launch |

<br/>

## ✨ Komi aesthetic

Yuko's interface is an adaptation of the **Manga** *personality* from [Komi Store](https://github.com/komi-store/komi-store), a design system that drops Material to look like a manga page instead:

- 📄 **Paper and ink**: cream background by day, ink black by night; no neutral grays.
- ▭ **Zero rounded corners**: panels with 3 dp borders and hard, offset shadows, no blur.
- 🔤 **Anton in uppercase** for headlines, Noto Sans for body text, JetBrains Mono for data.
- 🩹 **Stamps and screentones**: headers with a slanted marker, dot screentones in the corners, buttons that get "stamped" when pressed.
- 🎌 Japanese *kickers* in every section (今日 · FOR YOU, 本棚 · LIBRARY).

The colors are not Komi's: each theme takes its palette from a Touhou Project character (Reimu, Marisa, Patchouli, Sakuya, Remilia, Flandre, Cirno, Youmu, Yuyuko, Alice), each with a day and a night version.

## 💔 Why Yuko exists

This is not an app "to avoid paying." It is a response to a concrete problem: **in Latin America, legal manga arrives late, expensive, and in dribs and drabs.**

- 📚 **The legal catalog is a fraction.** Publishers releasing in Spanish license a small selection of what comes out in Japan, years behind, with series cut off midway when they stop selling. Thousands of titles have no Spanish edition at all, and many scanlations exist precisely because nobody has brought them over.
- 💸 **One volume costs as much as a family meal.** Importing manga into the region means paying tariffs, shipping, and distribution; the final price ends up far above what a young reader on the local minimum wage can afford.
- 📱 **Official digital platforms barely exist in Spanish.** Shueisha's MANGA Plus is the honorable exception, free and with simulpub. Almost everything else is in English or Japanese, priced in dollars, and not licensed for the region.
- ☠️ **And people already read on dangerous sites.** Pirate manga websites live off invasive ads, pop-ups, cryptominers, and malware. Yuko at least removes that layer: no ads, no trackers, nothing weird to install.

Yuko doesn't fix any of that. It only makes the path people already use cleaner and safer while the industry fails to show up.

## 🙇 To the authors, assistants, and publishers

We apologize. Sincerely, not as a formality.

Behind every chapter there is a mangaka and their assistants working seventy-plus-hour weeks for a paycheck that depends on the collected volume, and behind every Spanish edition there are translators, letterers, and small publishers betting their own money. Every read that doesn't go through an official channel is a read that never comes back to them. We know that. That is why this project has no advertising, makes no money, and doesn't try to compete with anyone: if tomorrow the whole catalog reached the region legally, Yuko should disappear, and we would be fine with that.

<br/>

## 💚 Support manga for real

If you use Yuko, **give something back**. It doesn't take much; it has to be real. In order of impact:

1. 📚 **Buy the official volumes** of what you love most, when they exist in your country. It's the most direct income for the author and the publisher, and what decides whether the series keeps being published.
2. 📲 **Use MANGA Plus** and the official platforms when they carry the title. It's free, it's legal, and every read counts for the author.
3. 🧸 **Official merchandise, not knockoffs.** A licensed figure or artbook pays salaries; the imitation pays someone else.
4. 🏪 **Buy from local bookstores and comic shops.** They are the ones asking publishers to bring more titles.
5. 📣 **Ask for what you want to read, through official channels.** Spanish-language publishers pick their licenses by looking at those requests.
6. 💬 **Talk about the work, not the piracy site.** Recommend the series, the author, the publisher. Let demand grow for the legal option, not for the shortcut.

And if a manga you read on Yuko gets a legal edition in your country, **switch**. That's the deal.

## 🔧 How it works

```
Yuko
├── app/              Compose UI (home, library, catalog, details, reader, downloads, settings)
└── manga-sources/    Runtime for the Kotatsu parsers: networking, cookies, Cloudflare, JavaScript, configuration, registry
```

- 🧩 **Compiled parsers, not extensions.** The Kotatsu parser library is a regular dependency; Yuko exposes the Spanish-language sources that aren't flagged as broken and gives them what they expect from their host (`MangaLoaderContext`).
- 🌐 **Shared HTTP client.** A single OkHttp using the WebView's cookies, so a Cloudflare challenge solved in the WebView works for both requests and images.
- 📖 **Our own reader.** Right-to-left by default, fit to screen height with horizontal paging, pinch to zoom, double tap, side zones to turn the page, and vertical mode for webtoons. Progress is saved per page.
- 📦 **Our own downloads.** Each chapter is saved as a folder of numbered pages; several workers in parallel, free-space checks, and per-page resume.

### Build

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Chidaruma696/Yuko.git
```

## 📢 Keep Android Open

> **Your phone is about to stop being yours.** [keepandroidopen.org](https://keepandroidopen.org/)

In 2025 Google announced **mandatory developer verification**, effective in 2027: anyone publishing an Android app will have to register in a central Google system, pay a fee, and hand over their government ID. Apps from anyone who doesn't register **will be blocked on every certified device in the world**, whether or not they are on the Play Store, F-Droid's included. Installing on your own will become a nine-step process with a 24-hour wait, controlled by Google Play Services and revocable at any time.

Let's be clear: **if that goes into effect, Yuko ceases to exist.** So does Watanuki, and any free app that doesn't go through Google's checkout. This project is only possible because Android is still open.

That is why the app shows a notice on the Home screen (it can be hidden) and a permanent link under Settings › About. The campaign is backed by 71 organizations from 23 countries (F-Droid, EFF, FSF, Nextcloud, Proton, KDE, Tor Project, LineageOS, GNOME, Brave…). What it asks for is simple:

- 📲 **Install F-Droid** on every Android device you own.
- ✍️ **Sign the petition** and share the page.
- 🏛️ **Write to your competition or consumer protection regulator.**
- 🧑‍💻 If you're a developer: **don't register**, and convince others not to.
